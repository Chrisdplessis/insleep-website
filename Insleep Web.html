Serving Potch since 1960

A Bult institution

Insleep has fed Potchefstroom's Bult for generations — long before it was a student postcode. What started as a neighbourhood convenience store grew into a takeaway landmark: pizza ovens next to the milk fridge, soft serve next to the till.

The idea hasn't changed — be close, be open, be quick — for the students, residence staff and locals who need something good to eat at an hour when most kitchens have shut.
